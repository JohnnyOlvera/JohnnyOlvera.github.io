The Producer’s Director

Creative Vision. Production Discipline.

Johnny Olvera is a writer and director whose work is shaped by a rare combination of performance experience, visual ambition and practical production knowledge.

A SAG-AFTRA actor since childhood, he has spent more than three decades studying how performance is built from inside a scene. Twelve years as a script supervisor gave him an equally deep understanding of coverage, continuity, scheduling and the consequences every creative decision carries across a production.

That perspective defines his approach to directing: protect the actor, clarify the story and design the production so the intended film can actually be delivered.

His feature directorial debut, THE WARREN, was filmed across more than 30 locations in three days for $3,200 and became an Official Nominee at the SAG-AFTRA American Scene Awards.

Today, through Goldwood Cinema, Johnny develops character-driven film and television projects across drama, thriller, science fiction and elevated genre storytelling.

His work begins with emotion—but it is built to withstand production.
